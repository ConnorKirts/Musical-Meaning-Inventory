# MMI

This repository provides data and analyses for a study titled "The Musical Meaning Inventory: Development and Validation of a State-Based Measure of Musical Meanings"

The MMI was developed to identify the likely broad areas of musical meaning that people consider to impact their specific situation and context.

This state-based measure has been developed to compliment and further describe emotional episodes experienced with music, as outlined in the Episode Model ([Eerola, Kirts & Saarikallio, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1177/03057356241279763)).

The items used for the MMI were operationalised and assessed by experts ([Kirts et al., 2026](https://doi.org/10.1177/20592043251413550)) prior to the dimensonality tests reported here.
